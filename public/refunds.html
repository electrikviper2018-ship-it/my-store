Refund Policy

Last updated:

Everything at FERMO is printed to order, so we cannot resell returned items. Here is how we handle problems.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your item arrives damaged, has a print defect, or is not what you ordered, email us within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a clear photo. We will send a free replacement or refund you in full, your choice. You do not need to return the item.

Changed your mind or wrong size

Because each item is made just for you, we cannot accept returns or give refunds for a change of mind or ordering the wrong model. Please check your phone model before you order.

Cancelling an order

Orders go into production quickly. Email us as soon as possible: if production has not started we will cancel and refund you in full. Once production has started, we cannot cancel.

Late or lost orders

If your order has not arrived when the tracking says it should, or tracking shows it lost, email us and we will investigate and reship or refund.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to your original payment method. They can take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on your bank.
