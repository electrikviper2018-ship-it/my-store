Cookie Policy

Last updated:

This page explains what this site stores in your browser.

What we use

Cart storage (essential). Your cart contents are saved in your browser's local storage so they are still there if you refresh or come back. This stays on your device, and we do not use it to track you.

Stripe (essential). When you check out, you are taken to Stripe's payment page. Stripe may set its own cookies there to process payments and prevent fraud. See Stripe's privacy policy for details.

What we do not use

We do not use analytics, advertising, social media pixels or other tracking cookies. Because we only use essential storage, we do not show a cookie consent banner. If we add non-essential tracking in the future, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

Fonts

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. This is not a cookie, but your browser contacts Google to get them, which shares your IP address. See our Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can clear local storage and cookies in your browser settings at any time. That empties your cart.
