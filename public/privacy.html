Privacy Policy

Last updated:

FERMO is operated by ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information we collect when you buy from this store, and what we do with it.

What we collect

Only what we need to take payment and deliver your order: your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and the items you ordered. Payments are handled by Stripe, so we never see or store your full card number. We do not have customer accounts or a newsletter, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

How we use it

To process and ship your order, send order and delivery updates, answer your questions, handle refunds, prevent fraud, and meet tax, accounting and legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

Who receives it

Stripe processes your payment. Printify and the print provider and delivery carrier it assigns receive your name, address, email and phone number so they can make and deliver your order. Render hosts this website and, like most hosts, receives your IP address and standard request logs when you visit. Google Fonts serves this site's typefaces, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads, which shares your IP address with Google. We may also disclose information when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep order records as long as needed for tax, accounting, fraud prevention and dispute handling, then delete or anonymize them. Third parties keep data under their own policies.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, and to correct or delete it, by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Virginia, the UK or the EU), you may have additional legal rights, and we will honor the ones that apply to you. We will not treat you differently for asking. Some records must be kept for legal reasons.

Children

This store is not directed to children or teens, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Payments and checkout run over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affecting you occurs, we will notify you as the law requires.
